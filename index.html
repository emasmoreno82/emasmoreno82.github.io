Open Bar

Multiplayer Truco Simulator with Voice Recognition

Indie Game / Beta

Immersive digital adaptation of the classic Argentine Truco. It combines a stylized visual environment with innovative mechanics, allowing you to play in the traditional way or through real voice commands, replicating the authentic experience of "calling" the moves into the microphone.

Main Features

  • Real-Time Voice System: Python script dedicated, connected via UDP to process local audio and interpret intentions and volume (shouting).
  • Multiplayer Architecture: Native network flow (ENetMultiplayerPeer) syncing Lobby, Avatars, and Table without latency.
  • Rules Engine and AI: Strict digital referee (envido calculations, points at 24/30 flat) and Probability-based Artificial Intelligence for local mode.

Tecnologías

Godot Engine 4 GDScript Python Redes UDP / P2P
