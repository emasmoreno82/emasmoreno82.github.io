Open Bar

Indie game · Beta

Multiplayer Truco with voice recognition.

A digital version of the Argentine card game Truco. You can play with the mouse, or do it the proper way and call the plays into your microphone.

  • Voice system: a dedicated Python script talks to the game over UDP, processes local audio, and picks up both what you say and how loud you say it.
  • Multiplayer: Godot's ENetMultiplayerPeer keeps the lobby, avatars and table in sync.
  • Rules engine and AI: a strict referee (envido counting, points at 24/30) and a probability-based opponent for local mode.

Built with Godot 4 / GDScript / Python / UDP and P2P networking
